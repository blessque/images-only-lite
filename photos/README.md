# Put your photographs here

Any `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif`, `.tif` or `.gif`. They are resized for you — drop in
the full-size file.

**Order** — number them: `01-…`, `02-…`, `03-…`. That is the order they appear in.

**Size** — end the filename with:

| suffix | what it does |
|---|---|
| `-solo` | takes a whole row on its own |
| `-tight` | packs more images into its row |
| *(nothing)* | shares a row normally |

**Alt text** is taken from the filename: `01-sunrise-over-the-bay-solo.jpg` becomes
"Sunrise over the bay". A camera dump like `IMG_4821.jpg` gets none, which is better than
alt text that says "IMG 4821".

Delete a file to remove the photograph.
